How to Play

  • Place modules onto the 8x8 grid during the PLAYING phase.
  • Three deterministic modules are active in the tray at once.
  • Shielded tiles protect against spreading hazards and leaks.
  • Win: survive winTurns turns.
  • Loss: Integrity 0 or Pressure 100.

Module quick brief

  • Brace: deploy bracing to lock down space.
  • Shield Core: 1x1 core that shields adjacent tiles.
  • Purge: clears hazards in a 3x3 zone.
  • Pump: lowers pressure immediately.
  • Cycler: rerolls the active tray modules.
